## What the study involves
You will use **one** version of a healthcare directory in an isolated study environment and complete **5 short tasks** (~20–30 min estimated total). These include search, contact lookup, saving a hospital, career-reference tracking and a staff observation using study fixtures. Screen/audio recording depends on your consent and the approved protocol. Afterwards you complete two questionnaires (SUS, NASA-TLX) and an interview.
